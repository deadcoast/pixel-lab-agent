## Baseline and ordering

The queue family's PixelLab production and scoped maintainer destination acceptance are established; native host reliability and broader quality remain open. Existing request preparation, exact criteria handoff, allocation/update mechanisms, retention recovery and lightweight runtime packaging are implemented. They are foundations to extend, not tasks to recreate. See [audit scope](docs/IDENTIFIED.md#evidence-and-verification-scope) and [current TODO](TODO.md).

Milestones follow the current TODO order. M1's review/evidence/nomination, focused choice/comparison and everyday guidance slices are implemented; native outcomes and interventions are recorded separately. M1 remains open while native continuation/role qualification proceeds through the linked M2 work. M2 retains broader native qualification. M3 extends actual allocation-to-consumer production. M4 qualifies expertise/recovery. M5 finishes reusable distribution and support claims. A later milestone's enabling task may be done earlier when it directly removes an active dependency; record the link and retain its original milestone. Do not stop useful local work because one host is temporarily unavailable.

**Status:** partially demonstrated for the queue family; broader outcome open. **Existing work:** TODO group 3 and PX-PLAR-002; PX-PROD-001.
