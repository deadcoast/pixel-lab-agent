# Shoreline and planted-idle diagnostic consumer

These are authored controlled diagnostic pixels, not generated production art.
The destination is the shipped browser consumer at native 1x and nearest 3x.
The two domain families are independent; human acceptance is unperformed.

## Terrain intent
Sixteen 16x16 opaque cells use this project's binary corner convention:
NW, NE, SE, SW have weights 1, 2, 4, 8. Green is land and navy is water.
The authored vertex grids define shared material at adjacent corners. Inspect
both mixed shoreline and island neighborhoods for broken material joins.
This quadrant pattern is diagnostic geometry, not a universal tile art rule.

## Character intent
Four 16x16 transparent poses form a planted idle: feet retain the same ground
contact throughout arm motion. Per-frame pivots [8,14] meet anchor [20,28] in a
40x36 viewport. Preserve uneven durations [120,180,120,240] ms. The looping idle
wraps; the one-shot settle stops at its final pose. Judge apparent floor contact,
not only rectangle placement. Do not infer pleasant motion from a low difference
score or claim subjective live viewing from a still capture.

## Review boundaries
Mechanical image dimensions, pixel delivery, mapping, timing, pivots and scaling
are separate from visual continuity and usefulness. Inspect actual pixels at
native scale and enlarged context. Preserve valid unrelated members when revising.
A renderer pass neither creates artistic acceptance nor proves real generated art
quality. CF-09 must carry source/reference intent through actual returned art,
identify a useful correction, revise it and inspect the exact successor in context.
