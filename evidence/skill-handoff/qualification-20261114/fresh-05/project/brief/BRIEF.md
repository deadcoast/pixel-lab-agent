# Character family brief

Recorded 2026-11-14 from the project owner's instruction to PIXL. The "Owner instruction" section quotes the owner verbatim; the other sections are the specialist's reading of the supplied references and the status of each point.

## Owner instruction (verbatim)

Make a character family matching this project, using PIXL. The supplied references disagree about outline treatment and I have not chosen the final canvas size. The family will have a scout and a medic for a side-view game. References are reference.png and reference-b.png. Begin the project and make the progress possible in this session.

## Requirements established by the instruction

- The deliverable is a character family with two members: a scout and a medic.
- The game view is side-view.
- The family must match the supplied references (reference.png, reference-b.png).
- The two references disagree about outline treatment; the owner has not chosen between them.
- The final canvas size has not been chosen.
- No generation provider is assigned to this evaluation project; only local PIXL operations are available in this session.

## Reference observations (measured, not adopted)

Both files are 32x32 PNG with binary alpha, one opaque 4-connected component, identical visible bounding box (7,3)-(25,28), 390 visible pixels, and the same silhouette: a rounded-rectangle body with two 4px-wide ear-like protrusions on top and two 3x4 cream eye blocks.

- reference.png uses 3 colors: a continuous 2px dark outline (#25354A), a teal body fill (#56B5BC) and cream eyes (#F4DDA2). Outline treatment: continuous dark outline.
- reference-b.png uses 2 colors: teal body (#56B5BC) filling the whole silhouette, and the same cream eyes. Outline treatment: no outline; the silhouette edge is the body color.

These are observations. The 32x32 canvas, the exact hex values, binary alpha and the single-component construction are reference properties, not confirmed project rules.

## Open owner decisions

1. Outline treatment: continuous dark outline (reference.png) or no outline (reference-b.png). A third option, selective/partial outline, is possible but was not supplied.
2. Final canvas size for the characters.

## Specialist proposals (not adopted)

- Use the scout as the representative sample before producing the medic, so one reviewed pose settles outline, palette roles and proportions for both members.
- Treat the teal body and cream eye colors as color roles carried from the references; exact hex matching is a proposal, not a rule, until the owner confirms a fixed palette.
- The sample canvas should be chosen with the intended display scale and the side-view game's tile or screen metrics; 32x32 is the reference size and is one candidate, not a default.
