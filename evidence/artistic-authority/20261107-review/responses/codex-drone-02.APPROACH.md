## Review and revision plan

Review one representative sample before extending a family. Verify 16×16 dimensions mechanically. Inspect the actual two-pixel frame, teal body, and both detached antenna islands visually; use pixel measurements to resolve uncertain thickness or contact. Component count alone cannot establish those features.

Compare native and integer-enlarged views against the reference. Judge facial readability, frame dominance, useful interior space, antenna separation, and color hierarchy. Test light and dark backgrounds as diagnostics. Exact coordinates, gap-row count, total components, and palette membership are not production rejection checks. Engine import and display-scale acceptance remain unverified until a consumer is supplied.

If a sample crowds its eyes against the frame, simplify or reposition the interior marks while retaining the chosen canvas and frame. If antennae merge, restore their separation. If colors obscure the face or silhouette, revise contrast without inventing a palette lock. A failed sample establishes that attempt’s defect; it does not prove that a provider cannot satisfy the brief. Direct pixel correction is a proposed fallback for that attempt, not a reason to replace the user’s aesthetic.
