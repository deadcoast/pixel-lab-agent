## Transfer assessment

Silhouette review is applicable to these two gameplay roles. Actual references and their masks are available, but intended display scale, representative game backgrounds, final dimensions and confirmed role landmarks are missing. The references share exactly the same silhouette, so they cannot prove role differentiation. Native and integer-enlarged review can inspect clusters; diagnostic backgrounds cannot prove readability in the unseen game.

Edge-rhythm is secondary. The references use intentional rectangular corners and upper projections; there is no demonstrated stair-step defect to fix. Do not smooth them merely to regularize a contour. The selected method cannot resolve dark versus turquoise edge preference. That choice requires a representative art comparison and project direction.

The packaged `key-silhouette-landmarks` receipt checks a 16×16 key's hole, shaft and teeth plus a negative example. Its own limitation states that visual recognition, background readability and artistic acceptance require inspection. It is not evidence about a scout or medic. The `edge-rhythm-detour` receipt tests a declared unit-step diagonal and intentional versus accidental detours; it explicitly does not require organic contours to follow that pattern or auto-smooth artwork. Neither fixture was rerun or presented as project proof in this session.
